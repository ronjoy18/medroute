HOME CARE SERVICES

Your Loved One Deserves Quality Care. You Deserve Peace of Mind.

Caring for a loved one with a long-term condition, disability, or reduced mobility can be overwhelming. When caregiving becomes a full-time responsibility, you shouldn't have to carry it all by yourself.

Personalized Home-Based Support

At Medroute Errands, we connect families with attentive home-based caregivers who assist with daily routines while documenting your loved one's wellness:

  • • Personal Care: Assistance with bathing, dressing, personal hygiene, and comfortable daily routines.
  • • Mobility Support: Safe transfers, guided walking, and positional support tailored to client safety.
  • • Medication Reminders: Prompting timely intake of prescribed medication based on doctor care plans.
  • • Vital Signs Monitoring: Measuring and logging blood pressure, pulse, temperature, and blood oxygen levels ($SpO_2$).
  • • Daily Care Reports: Documenting observations, daily mood, and health updates to ensure care continuity.
  • • Physician Coordination: Sharing care sheets with physicians to support informed clinical adjustments.
