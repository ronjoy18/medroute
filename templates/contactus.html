Caregiver Services

Your Loved One Deserves Quality Care. You Deserve Peace of Mind. Caring for a loved one with a long-term health condition, disability, or reduced mobility can be overwhelming. You don't have to do it alone. When caring for your loved ones becomes a full-time responsibility, who takes care of everything else?

Between helping your loved one bathe, get dressed, move safely, and manage daily routines, it can be difficult to find time for work, family, or even yourself. You may worry about their well-being when you need to step away, wondering whether their needs are being met and their condition is being monitored. You shouldn't have to choose between caring for someone you love and taking care of your own responsibilities. At Medroute Errands, we connect families with home-based caregivers who provide personalized support tailored to each client's needs. Our services include:

  • Personal care: Assistance with bathing, dressing, personal hygiene, and other daily activities.
  • Mobility support: Helping clients move safely and comfortably according to their individual needs.
  • Medication reminders: Helping clients follow their prescribed medication schedules, as directed in their care plans.
  • Vital signs monitoring: Measuring and recording agreed-upon vital signs, such as blood pressure, pulse, temperature, and oxygen saturation, where appropriate.
