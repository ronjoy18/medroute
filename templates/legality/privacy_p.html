1. Introduction & Data Controller

At Medroute Errands ("Medroute", "we", "us", or "our"), we value your privacy and are committed to providing a secure and transparent experience across our website (www.medrouterrands.com)[cite: 1]. We operate platforms connecting clients with home-based caregivers, non-emergency clinical transport, and specialist doctors. This policy outlines how we process personal data and deploy cookies in strict adherence to Kenya’s Data Protection Act (2019) and international clinical standards[cite: 1].

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to coordinate care, process billing, and comply with clinical safety regulations:

  • Account Identifiers: Name, phone number, email address, password hashes, and user roles.
  • Care & Errand Directives: Mobility assistance requirements, scheduling routines, hospital queuing instructions, and caregiver notes.
  • Billing Records: Transaction codes, M-Pesa mobile money references, and payment amounts (we do not store personal M-Pesa PINs or bank card secrets).
  • Technical Identifiers: IP addresses, browser information, and necessary session markers to protect against unauthorized account access.

3. Cookies Policy & Usage

[cite: 1]

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you browse our pages[cite: 1]. They help our website remember certain session details, verify logins, and understand how visitors interact with our services[cite: 1]:

• Essential & Security Cookies: Required for core functionality, CSRF forgery protection (csrftoken), and account authentication (sessionid)[cite: 1]. These operate automatically without prior consent[cite: 1].
• Functional Cookies: Remember user preferences such as your saved cookie choices (medroute_cookie_choice)[cite: 1].
• Performance & Analytics Cookies: Help measure visitor traffic and technical page performance[cite: 1]. These are gated and disabled until you explicitly click "Accept All Cookies" on our banner[cite: 1].

Note on Health Privacy: We do not use cookies to collect clinical diagnoses, patient appointment logs, or sensitive health data[cite: 1]. You may update your cookie choices at any time via the Cookie Preferences control in the footer[cite: 1].

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

Personal data is processed strictly under the following lawful parameters:

  • Contractual Performance: To schedule home-care sessions, dispatch errand assistants, and issue digital receipts.
  • Consent: For non-essential analytics tracking and optional email updates[cite: 1].
  • Legal & Clinical Duty: Maintaining verifiable logs of non-emergency medical accompaniment as required by safety oversight.

5. Third-Party Sub-Processors

We collaborate with vetted technical service providers under strict data privacy agreements[cite: 1]:

  • Safaricom Daraja API: Mobile money payment settlement and STK verification.
  • Cloud Hosting & Storage: Cloud database servers with encrypted HTTPS/TLS transmissions and AES storage encryption.
  • Google Analytics (Optional): Gated analytics tracking activated only upon user consent[cite: 1].
