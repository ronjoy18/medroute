Privacy Policy & Data Processing Notice

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Revised: October 2026

1. Introduction & Data Controller

Medroute Systems ("Medroute", "we", "us", or "our") operates platforms providing clinical errands, home caregiving coordination, and specialist tele-referrals. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your personal and health-related data in strict adherence to Kenya’s Data Protection Act (2019) and international clinical confidentiality frameworks.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to coordinate care, process billing, and comply with safety regulations:

  • Account Identifiers: Name, phone number, email address, password hashes, and national identification details.
  • Health & Care Directives: Specific mobility requirements, medication schedules, and clinical coordinator notes provided during onboarding.
  • Financial Records: Transaction codes, M-Pesa receipts, and billing history (we do not store raw card numbers or M-Pesa PINs).
  • Technical Logs: IP addresses, browser agents, and necessary session markers to protect against unauthorized account takeovers.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal data exclusively under the following legal frameworks:

  • Contractual Performance: To schedule caregivers, dispatch errand couriers, and generate booking invoices.
  • Explicit Consent: For tracking analytics cookies and optional informational notifications. Consent may be revoked at any time.
  • Legal Compliance: To maintain verifiable audit trails for medical transport and diagnostic referrals.

4. Cookie Classification & User Rights

Our platform categorizes cookies into two tiers:

Strictly Necessary: Django session tokens (`sessionid`) and CSRF tokens (`csrftoken`). These are required for login and security verification and do not record marketing profiles.

Performance & Analytics: Optional tags deployed only after explicit user approval. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie Preferences link in the footer.

5. Third-Party Sub-Processors

We partner with vetted technical infrastructure providers that uphold industry-standard data security:

  • Safaricom Daraja API: Mobile money payment processing.
  • Cloud Hosting & DB: Encrypted databases hosted on secure cloud infrastructure with SSL/TLS transmission in transit and AES-256 at rest.

6. Exercising Your Data Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, you have the right to request access to your records, object to processing, or request account erasure. To submit a formal inquiry, contact our Data Protection Desk at privacy@medroute.com.
