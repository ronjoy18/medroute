About Medroute Errands

Bridging Families, Caregivers, & Healthcare Providers

When someone you love needs ongoing care, managing daily needs, monitoring health, arranging hospital visits, and staying in touch with their physician can quickly become overwhelming. At Medroute Errands, we ensure families never walk this journey alone.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, compassionate, and coordinated home-based care and medical support services that improve patients’ well-being and give families peace of mind[cite: 2].

Our Vision

To become a trusted leader in integrated home-based care and medical support services in Kenya, transforming how patients, families, caregivers, and healthcare professionals connect and collaborate to deliver accessible, compassionate, and patient-centred care[cite: 2].

About Medroute Errands

Based in Kenya, Medroute Errands is a medical support and care-coordination agency that connects families with home-based caregivers, nurses, drivers, and patients’ physicians[cite: 2]. Our services support people living with long-term health conditions, disabilities, or limited mobility who need consistent care, monitoring, and help accessing healthcare[cite: 2].

We bring together the people and services involved in your loved one's care, ensuring nothing important is managed in isolation[cite: 2].

How We Make a Difference

1. Home-Based Care That Keeps You Informed[cite: 2]

We connect your family with home-based caregivers who assist your loved one with personal care, daily activities, and mobility[cite: 2]. Our nursing team reviews daily caregiver logs and compiles weekly reports to coordinate follow-ups with the client's physician[cite: 2].

2. Medical Appointments Without the Added Stress[cite: 2]

From routine consultations to regular dialysis visits, we arrange comfortable transport, provide caregiver accompaniment, assist with hospital errands, and ensure your loved one returns home safely[cite: 2].

3. Access to Licensed Medical Specialists[cite: 2]

Through our verified network, you can explore available medical professionals across different clinical specialties and connect directly with a verified physician suited to your loved one’s specific diagnostic needs[cite: 2].

4. One Connected Approach to Care[cite: 2]

Care involves family members, caregivers, nurses, drivers, and doctors[cite: 2]. Our integrated digital platform synchronizes updates and clinical observations to support seamless continuity of care[cite: 2].

Workflow Architecture

How Medroute Coordinates Care

A closed-loop system connecting patient homes, deployed caregivers, internal nursing officers, medical specialists, and hospital facilities[cite: 2].

1. Field Deployment & Vitals

Caregivers deployed to client homes capture daily observations and report back to Medroute nursing leads[cite: 2].

2. Specialist Triage Loop

Medroute compiles weekly summaries, transmits them to doctors, and returns refined instructions to families[cite: 2].

3. Assisted Transit & Errands

Direct door-to-door coordination for lab collections, prescription pickups, and hospital check-ups[cite: 2].
